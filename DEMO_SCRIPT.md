# Demo video script (~2 minutes)

**Setup before recording:** open the deployed site with `?demo` at the end of the URL so sample data is loaded. Use a screen recorder (on Mac: press **Cmd + Shift + 5** → "Record Entire Screen"). Optionally record your voice, or add captions afterwards.

| Time | Show on screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Dashboard | "Hi, I'm ___, an informatics student in Taiwan. I just bought my first motorcycle, a SYM Wild Wolf 150, and I had so many questions that I built **MotoMate** — a co-pilot for motorcycle riders in Taiwan." |
| 0:15–0:35 | Dashboard: point at the odometer, ride score, "Up next" and Paperwork cards | "The dashboard shows my bike, today's ride score, which maintenance is due next, and countdowns for Taiwan paperwork like compulsory insurance and the fuel fee." |
| 0:35–0:55 | Garage: scroll the cards, click **Done** on *Chain lube*, see it turn green | "The Garage has a maintenance schedule for my bike. Each item is tracked by both kilometers and time. My chain is overdue — I lube it, tap Done, and it's green again." |
| 0:55–1:15 | Fuel: hover over the charts, click **Add fill-up** and add one | "Every time I fill up, I log it, and MotoMate calculates my real fuel economy — about 41 km per liter — and how much I spend each month." |
| 1:15–1:40 | Ride or Rain?: switch city to Kaohsiung, then Hualien; scroll the 12-hour strip | "Ride or Rain uses live weather and air-quality data to give a ride score from 0 to 100. It explains the score, finds the best time to ride, and tells me what to bring — like a raincoat or a PM2.5 mask." |
| 1:40–1:55 | Diagnose: *Won't start* → answer 3 questions → results | "If something's wrong, Diagnose asks a few questions and ranks the likely causes, with DIY tips, repair cost in NT dollars and safety warnings." |
| 1:55–2:10 | Shrink the browser window or show the phone view | "It works on any phone, needs no account, and all data stays private. Next I want to add Chinese, AI photo diagnosis and GPS ride tracking. Thanks for watching!" |
